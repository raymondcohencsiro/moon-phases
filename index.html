The tilt turns slowly as the Earth goes round the Sun, so it lines up with the Sun about every 6 months ("eclipse seasons"). Playing the animation turns it too.

Drag the orbit view to rotate it, scroll or pinch to zoom. Drag the Moon to move it around its orbit. Double-click the Sun, Earth or Moon to centre on it.
